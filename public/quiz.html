How to Play QuizRoom 💡

Modes:

  • Solo Mode: Practice answering questions against the clock to build knowledge and speed.
  • Room Multiplayer: Host a live game room with up to 8 players. Share the 6-character room code or link to let friends join.

Scoring Rules:

  • Correct answers earn up to 1,000 points per question.
  • Speed matters: The faster you pick the correct answer, the more points you receive!
  • Streak Bonuses: Consecutive correct answers award extra bonus points!
